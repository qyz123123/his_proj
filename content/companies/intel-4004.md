### Intel 4004: The Tiny Chip That Made Personal Computing Imaginable

By *Yunzhe Qu*

The first computer, **ENIAC**, weighed over 16 pounds, filled 5 rooms, and cost $7 million to build.

A personal computer? That was just a concept—unless you had a truck to carry ENIAC and a power station to run it. **(Historical Situation)**

**Intel 4004, the first microprocessor, completely changed that situation**.  Old computer circuits that once filled rooms were carved onto a tiny chip smaller than your fingertip. What made it outstanding was not only its size, but also its price, **costing less than $100**. 

Computers made with INTEL 4004 could fit on a desk, making personal computers both affordable and feasible.
