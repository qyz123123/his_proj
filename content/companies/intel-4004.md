# Intel 4004: The Tiny Chip That Made Personal Computing Imaginable

By *Yunzhe Qu*

The first computer, **ENIAC**, weighed over 16 pounds, filled 5 rooms, and cost $7 million to build.

A personal computer? That was just a concept—unless you had a truck to carry ENIAC and a power station to run it.

BUT, **INTEL 4004 IS DIFFERENT**

Old computer circuits that once filled rooms were carved onto a tiny chip smaller than your fingertip. **This made personal computers feel possible.**

The chip **costs less than $100**. Computers made with INTEL 4004 could fit on a desk, making personal computers both affordable and feasible.
