### MacBook Air: The Laptop That Allowed Everyone To Carry A Computer Without Feeling Annoyed

***There Is Something In the Air***

With only an envelope filled with MacBook Air, he demonstrates that MacBook Air is the "thinnest laptop in the world." **(Point of View)**

That point of view impressed the world, and leveled Apple Computers to the highest level of all brands of computer. It completely changed people's view on laptops, which are previous heavy, noisy, and thick. A computer on a desk is useful. But a computer you can carry everywhere changes your habits.

**MacBook Air Made Portability Feel Normal**

**If a computer is easier to carry, people use it more often.** **If people use it more often, the computer becomes part of ordinary life.**
