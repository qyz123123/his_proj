### MacBook Air: The Laptop That Allowed Everyone To Carry A Computer Without Feeling Annoyed

By *Yunzhe Qu*

A computer on a desk is useful.

But a computer you can carry everywhere changes your habits.

Before thin laptops became common, many portable computers were heavy, noisy and thick. They could move, but they did not always feel natural to carry every day.

**MacBook Air Made Portability Feel Normal**

**If a computer is easier to carry, people use it more often.**

**If people use it more often, the computer becomes part of ordinary life.**

**MacBook Air helped make the personal computer feel like a daily companion.**
